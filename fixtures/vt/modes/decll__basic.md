# 1b 5b 31 71|CSI 1 q|DECLL, ignored / 78|x|text

DECLL (CSI Ps q) is ignored: the cursor style stays.

Reference: xterm ctlseqs: DECLL. Terminal size: 20x6.

| hex | bytes | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 1b 5b 32 20 71 | CSI 2 SP q | DECSCUSR |
| 1b 5b 31 71 | CSI 1 q | DECLL, ignored |
| 78 | x | text |
