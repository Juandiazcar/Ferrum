# halfwidth / katakana

Halfwidth Katakana (H) take one column.

Reference: UAX #11 (H). Terminal size: 20x6.

| hex | bytes | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| ef bd b1 ef bd b2 ef bd b3 | <ef> <bd> <b1> <ef> <bd> <b2> <ef> <bd> <b3> | text |
| e6 bc a2 | <e6> <bc> <a2> | text |
| ef bd b4 | <ef> <bd> <b4> | text |
